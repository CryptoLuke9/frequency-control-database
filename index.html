02 / Reading the archive

Evidence has
different weights.

The collection separates a case’s reported mechanism, its official account, and the interpretation supplied alongside it. Read all three. Follow the references.

01

On the record

Classified in the supplied archive as documented in primary or peer-reviewed material.

02

Contested

A documented case whose interpretation, methods, or conclusions are disputed in the supplied account.

03

Claimed / folklore

A claim or narrative included for context. Inclusion does not establish that the claim is true.

PROVENANCE / Case text, dates, classifications, and reference links come from the HTML supplied for this project. They have not been independently fact-checked for this edition. “The story” is the supplied editorial interpretation. Counts describe this collection, not the prevalence of research. Source coverage varies by entry.
